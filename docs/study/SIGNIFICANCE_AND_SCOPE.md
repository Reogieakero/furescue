# FurEscue — Significance, Scope and Limitations of the Study

## 1.4 Significance of the Study

This study will be beneficial to various stakeholders involved in animal welfare management and community participation in Mati City. The findings and the developed system are expected to support animal rescuers, volunteers, the City Veterinarian's Office, community members, and future researchers by providing a more organized, accessible, and technology-driven platform for animal rescue reporting, adoption management, and welfare monitoring.

**Animal Rescuers and Volunteers.** The system will help rescuers and volunteers manage animal-related reports more efficiently by providing a centralized platform. It will allow them to easily locate reported cases through mapping features, prioritize urgent situations, and respond more quickly. This will reduce delays and improve overall rescue operations.

**City Veterinarian.** The system will assist the City Veterinarian in organizing and monitoring animal welfare data. It will provide better visibility of areas with frequent incidents, helping them plan actions and allocate resources more effectively. This will lead to more organized and data-driven decision-making.

**Community Members.** Community members will benefit from a simple and accessible platform that allows them to report stray, injured, or abandoned animals and conveniently browse animals available for adoption. This system encourages active participation in animal welfare efforts, strengthens collaboration between the public and rescuers, and makes the adoption process more efficient, increasing the chances of animals finding safe and permanent homes.

**Future Researchers.** This study will serve as a reference for future projects related to animal welfare systems and community-based platforms. It will provide insights into developing systems that integrate mapping features and adoption services in one platform.

## 1.5 Scope and Limitations

### Scope

The scope of the system covers the following areas:

- The system will only be deployed in the City of Mati.
- The system currently supports adoption for dogs and cats only, specifically including both Aspin (Asong Pinoy) and Puspin (Pusang Pinoy) breeds.
- A mapping feature will be included to pin the exact location of stray or injured animals within the City of Mati.
- Provide secure user authentication and role-based access control ensuring that adopters, community reporters, and administrators have appropriate permissions and data protection.
- Users can browse available pets, view their detailed profiles, and submit adoption applications online.
- Community reporting system that allows residents to report stray, injured, or abandoned animals.
- Administrative dashboard for animal welfare organizations to manage rescue reports, adoption requests, and animal records efficiently.
- Geotagging integration to visualize reported animal cases through heatmaps, enabling identification of high-risk areas and supporting data-driven rescue interventions.
- Cross-platform accessibility through a responsive interface compatible with both web and mobile devices.
- Communication features such as in-app messaging to streamline coordination among stakeholders.
- Analytical reporting tools to generate statistics on adoption trends, rescue efficiency, and geographic distribution of cases.

### Limitations

The system is subjected to the following limitations:

- The system will initially be limited to Mati City and may not cover other municipalities.
- The system primarily operates online and requires internet connectivity for real-time synchronization and data access. However, reports may function asynchronously to minimize disruption during unstable connections.
- Actual rescue operations and animal care depend on the availability of manpower, facilities, and resources of partner organizations, which the system cannot directly provide.
- Reports submitted by community members rely on user input and may be subject to inaccuracies or incomplete information.
- The geotagging heatmap will focus on visualizing reported cases but will not include advanced predictive analytics or AI-driven forecasting in its initial version.
- While the system will implement authentication and role-based access, it will not cover advanced cybersecurity measures such as biometric verification or blockchain-based data storage.
- Messaging and notifications will be limited to system-generated alerts and basic communication; integration with third-party messaging apps (Messenger, WhatsApp) is not included.
- The system does not include AI-based animal recognition or health diagnosis features.
- Adoption approval processes are dependent on manual verification by administrators.
- The system will not process online payments for donations or adoption fees; all financial matters must be handled personally by the City Veterinarian.
