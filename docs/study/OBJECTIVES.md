# FurEscue — Objectives of the Study

## 1.3 Objectives of the Study

This project aims to improve the monitoring, reporting, and adoption of rescued animals in Mati City while supporting animal welfare organizations in managing rescue operations efficiently.

This study specifically aims to:

1. Create an online adoption portal that allows users to view available animals for adoption and submit adoption requests.
2. Implement a reporting system where community members can report stray, injured, or abandoned animals.
3. Integrate geotagging for heatmap visualization of reported animal cases.
4. Implement a notification system that informs adopters, rescuers, and administrators about updates on adoption requests, rescue operations, and reported cases in real time.
5. Generate descriptive analytical reports and statistics on adoption trends, rescue efficiency, and geographic distribution of cases.
6. Integrate communication features such as in-app messaging between adopters, rescuers, and administrators to streamline coordination.
7. Enable community members to post animals for adoption through the system.
8. Implement an e-learning module on responsible pet ownership, featuring educational content on dog and cat behavior and basic training to promote proper animal care and awareness among users.
9. Integrate a 3D profiling feature for animals to enhance visualization and support better identification in the adoption process.
10. Implement a health monitoring feature that records and tracks animals' medical history, vaccination status, and heart rate.
